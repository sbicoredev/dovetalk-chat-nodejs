# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, which is available under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The short version is below. Where it is silent, the
Covenant applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, and assume good faith.
- Be respectful of different views and of people's time. Critique the work, not the person.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and accept it the same way.
- Own mistakes, say sorry, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address, without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would call inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project
in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers privately: use the repository's **Report a vulnerability** form (it is private to
the maintainers and works for conduct reports too), or write to the owner at the address on their GitHub profile.
Reports are read promptly, treated in confidence, and the reporter is not identified to the person reported without
consent.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban a person for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They explain the reason when they do.
